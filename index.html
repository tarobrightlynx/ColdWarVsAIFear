2025–2026 AI finding

Overall fear intensity remained stable: 6.132 in 2025 and 6.121 in 2026.

Geopolitical Instability was the only category that remained significant after Holm adjustment, increasing from 18.1% to 38.3%. Surveillance was higher in 2025, but it did not remain significant after adjustment.

Methodology

1

Assemble corpora

60 Cold War documents and two AI datasets of 120 documents each.

2

Prepare texts

Remove scraper metadata, screen unusable records, and preserve source identifiers.

3

Repeated coding

Submit each document 25 times using the same fixed GPT-4o mini prompt.

4

Score fear

Assign a 1–10 fear-intensity rating and multi-label category coding.

5

Aggregate by source

Average repeated runs so each document remains one independent observation.

6

Compare groups

Use two-tailed Welch independent-samples t-tests.

7

Adjust categories

Apply Holm correction across the ten category comparisons.

8

Interpret carefully

Use p-values, confidence intervals, effect sizes, and substantive context together.

Key limitations

  • The corpora differ in time period, genre, geography, and collection method.
  • Risk-oriented purposive sampling does not represent all media coverage or the general public.
  • Repeated LLM runs measure model variability but do not establish validity.
  • The exploratory human review was not matched at the document level, so formal agreement statistics could not be calculated.
  • Category scores measure expressed fear in texts, not the psychological states of authors or readers.
